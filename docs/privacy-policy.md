# Privacy Policy for Unshelved

**Last updated:** May 11, 2026

## Overview

Unshelved is an open-source audiobook player that connects to your self-hosted Audiobookshelf server. The app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer or any third party.

## Data the App Handles

### Server Credentials

The app stores your Audiobookshelf server URL and authentication token locally on your device using encrypted storage. These credentials are used solely to communicate with your self-hosted server and are never shared with any third party.

### Playback Data

Listening progress, bookmarks, and playback history are synced exclusively between the app and your Audiobookshelf server. This data never leaves the connection between your device and your server.

### Downloaded Content

Audiobook files you download for offline listening are stored locally on your device. No copies are made elsewhere.

## Data Collection

Unshelved does **not**:

- Collect analytics or usage statistics
- Use advertising SDKs or tracking frameworks
- Transmit data to the developer or any third-party service
- Access contacts, location, camera, microphone, or other sensitive device features beyond what is required for audio playback

## Network Communication

The app communicates exclusively with the Audiobookshelf server URL you configure. No other network requests are made. All communication uses the protocol (HTTP/HTTPS) configured by you when entering your server address.

## Permissions

The app requests the following permissions:

| Permission                          | Purpose                                                   |
|-------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| Internet                            | Connect to your Audiobookshelf server                     |
| Foreground Service (Media Playback) | Continue audio playback when the app is in the background |
| Wake Lock                           | Prevent the device from sleeping during playback          |
| Post Notifications                  | Show playback controls in the notification area           |

## Data Retention

All data is stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data including credentials, preferences, and downloaded files.

## Children's Privacy

Unshelved does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app does not collect information from any user.

## Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy is updated, the revised version will be posted with an updated date at the top. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
